# EPIC-016: Policy Engine

## Goal
Design the Policy Engine, a governance architect that evaluates requests before execution to enforce strict constraints.

## Scope
Enforce: Technology constraints, Architecture constraints, Security policies, Naming conventions, Required documentation, Required reviews, Team policies, Compliance policies.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Designs Policy hierarchy and Evaluation order.
- [ ] Designs Override rules, Exception handling, and Policy inheritance.
- [ ] Designs Plugin support for custom policies.
