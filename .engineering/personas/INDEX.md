# AI Personas (INDEX)

This directory defines specialized cognitive filters (Personas) that AI Assistants can adopt. 

Rather than attempting to be a generalist, the AI reads a persona file during initialization. This persona instructs the AI to prioritize specific workflows, enforce distinct standards, and leverage particular cognitive engines, ensuring expert-level behavior tailored to the task.

- [Security Auditor](./security-auditor.md)
- [Backend Engineer](./backend-engineer.md)
- [Frontend Specialist](./frontend-specialist.md)
